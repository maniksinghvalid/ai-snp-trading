<objective>
Stop `bot/execution/engine.py` from swallowing a failed `cancel_order` at its four
cancel sites (see the table in CONTEXT.md). Today a raised cancel, such as an OpenD
rate limit on the paper account shared with the options bot, is treated as "order dead".
The engine then places the next BUY or SELL (a second live order, which can mean an
untracked long or an over-sell into a SHORT) or books a quantity that can still grow as
final. This is CR-04 parity with the options fix 260925-goi (`git show 2cb1d0f`).

Purpose: a correct and safe automated trade loop must never have two live orders for one
intent or position.
Output: TDD regression tests (RED first), the engine fix, and caller handling in bot.py,
manager.py and main.py. Every locked decision D1-D6 in 260925-ho6-CONTEXT.md is
implemented exactly as written.
</objective>
