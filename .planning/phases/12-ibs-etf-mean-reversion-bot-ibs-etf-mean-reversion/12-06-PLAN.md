<objective>
Build the core of `IbsBot` (IBS-04, IBS-05, IBS-07): construction with the watchdog duck-type contract, the startup
readiness gate, own-rows-only reconcile with the SAFE-OG-01 analog, and the daily decision job up to and including
the exit batch (exits are placed BEFORE entries — D-04; entries are Plan 08).

Purpose: this is the trade loop's safety core — nothing trades before reconcile, nothing outside the bot's own rows
is ever touched, and every decided exit is retried until flat.
