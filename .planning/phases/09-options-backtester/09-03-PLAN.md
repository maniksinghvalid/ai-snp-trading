<objective>
Build the daily replay engine and the options-shaped report glue: feed day-t rows through the
unmodified Phase 8 strategy functions, apply the live portfolio caps in the live order, fill at
mid ± slippage, manage and settle positions, and turn the resulting trade log into metrics.

Purpose: this is where OBT-01 (import-not-copy) becomes provable and where OBT-05's fill model and
OBT-07's metrics are defined. It is also the one place a subtle divergence from live would silently
invalidate every hypothesis verdict, so the gate order, the P&L formula and the row keys are
specified against exact line ranges in `bot/options/service.py` rather than re-derived.

Output: `backtester/options/{engine,report}.py` plus their tests.
</objective>
