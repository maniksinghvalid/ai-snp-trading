    - `iv_rank([...], window=252, min_obs=60)` on a hand-built series returns `(iv - min) / (max - min) * 100` over the trailing `window` observations, verified against a hand-computed value.

    - `iv_rank` returns `None` when fewer than `min_obs` observations exist (warm-up not satisfied) and when max == min (degenerate flat window).
    - Only the trailing `window` observations count: an extreme value older than `window` entries back does not change the result.
    - The returned number is in 0-100 percent units — feeding it as `u["ivr_pct"]` with `rules_options.json`'s `ivr_min: 30` passes at 31 and fails at 29 (assert against the real `passes_entry_gate`).
