<objective>
Establish the exit-model config surface (EXIT-MODEL, success criterion 5) without shipping any unvalidated exit behavior. Add `exit.model` to `rules.json` (enum of the three candidate models, defaulting to the current `partial_be_trail`), validate it in the schema, expose it as `StrategyConfig.exit_model`, and make the loader FAIL CLOSED for the two not-yet-implemented models so an operator cannot accidentally run an exit variant that has not been backtested.

Purpose: The exit model that ships must be chosen from Phase 6 backtest evidence (success criterion 2), but the Phase 6 backtester does not exist yet (RESEARCH Finding C-1). This plan does the safe, buildable half now: it turns the currently-implicit exit model into an explicit, config-driven, schema-validated choice (CFG-01), and installs the seam that plan 07-06 will complete once Phase 6 lands and the variant FSM branches are implemented and compared. Current live behavior is unchanged (default = partial_be_trail); the fixed_2r and full_to_1p5r_trail values are defined but fail closed until Phase 6.

Output: `exit.model` in rules.json; schema enum; `StrategyConfig.exit_model` + fail-closed loader guard; extended loader tests.
</objective>
