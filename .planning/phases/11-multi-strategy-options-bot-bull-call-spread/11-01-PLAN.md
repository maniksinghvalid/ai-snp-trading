existing consumer depends on. `rules_options.json` may now be a `strategies` array + shared
`risk`/`execution`/`service` blocks; `load_options_book` returns every strategy as a flat
`OptionsConfig`; `load_options_config(path, strategy=None)` keeps returning ONE flat config
(first strategy by default); `legacy_view` projects a strategy back to the legacy flat dict for
the Phase 9 backtester; every semantically-wrong shape fails closed with `ConfigError`.

Purpose: MSO-01/02/03 — the loader is the foundation every later plan builds on, and the D-26
no-drift guarantee for `tasty_credit_spreads` starts here (legacy files are wrapped into the
same mapping path, so there is ONE mapping, not two that can drift).
Output: schema.py STRATEGIES_SCHEMA, config.py book/loader/legacy_view/checks, conftest
fixtures, tests. The shipped `rules_options.json` is NOT touched here (plan 11-04).
</objective>
