<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| OpenD broker → bot | Broker query results (positions, order status) cross into risk decisions; a failed/empty result must not be trusted as truth |
| Market data feed → bot | 5m bar pushes drive entry/exit; loss of the feed after reconnect silently halts management |
| Scheduler → force-close | Job timing governs whether positions are held past close |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-06.2-01 | Denial of Service | gateway.reconcile_once (1.2) | mitigate | On ret != RET_OK / data None, skip the cycle and alert; never derive "externally closed" from a failed query (never wipe managed positions) |
| T-06.2-02 | Denial of Service | watchdog._on_reconnect (1.3) | mitigate | Re-register the bar handler on the new quote ctx after reconnect + log a verification event, so bar-driven management resumes |
