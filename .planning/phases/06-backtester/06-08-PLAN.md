<objective>
Close the exit-fill fabrication (WR-01) and add the force-close fill mechanic (CR-05, execution
side) in `backtester/execution.py`. Today `manage_exit` returns `int(qty)` with `exit_price=None`
appended to `exit_fills` even when `next_bar` yields nothing — a fake "full fill" that
`_trigger_stop_out` treats as a real close, corrupting the trade log. And there is no way to close a
position that is legitimately still open at end-of-day, because a force-close on the final bar has no
N+1 bar to fill against.

Purpose: make `SimulatedExecution` honour the live `_place_exit_order`/`force_close_all` contract —
a missing next bar means "no fill, stay open" (WR-01), and an explicit force-close fills at the last
observed price and returns the full quantity so `PositionManager.force_close_all` can mark the
position CLOSED (enabling Plan 09's EOD force-close and complete trade capture, BT-03).

Output: a corrected `backtester/execution.py` plus unit tests. No live `bot/` code is modified;
`manage_exit`'s public signature is unchanged (the force-close/normal distinction is a harness-set
mode flag, not a new parameter).
</objective>
